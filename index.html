About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Yonsei University studying computer architecture and system advised by Professor Won Woo Ro. My research interests include Computer Architecture, Compute eXpress Link (CXL) and Rack-scale Computing. I am particularly interested in CXL Fabric Attached Memory System. My current research is financially supported by SK hynix and the Brain Korea 21 Program, which is funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea.
